# I've finally completed one of English text books

## Overview
This infographic shows the source post as a 100-day English textbook completion journey. It keeps the main numbers and routine steps visible.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. What was completed.
2. How each chapter was studied.
3. Why completion mattered.
