It's feel like I've gotten a priceless triumph. I've finished one of English textbooks that I started 100 days ago.

[가브리엘 잉글리시 다이어리](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000218052168) is that book. This book covers 50 chapters prepared for daily routine. It's composed of 50 stories and I picked them at one by one.

It took me about 100 days to finish these chapters, which means I averagely read a chapter every other days.

Here's how I studied. **Each chapter took me about 40 ~ 60 minutes a day.** I didn't take the online course but stuck to solid everyday routine.

One chapter comprises with a short story and break down section for each sentence. There are various useful English phrase or expressions that was helpful to me.

Beginning with daily study, I listened to the [audio for that chapter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwDpVEKGZ3k&list=PLWGv_i60H2EyZTYsOrL4LFtbXmQuSNPJ-) twice without a subtitle on YouTube first. And then, I read the contents of the chapter and created Anki flash cards by the sentences in that story.

The front side of the card is Korean meaning of the sentence and the backside includes English sentence and some pictures of textbook which explains about phrases or vocabularies in sentence.

Finally, I did the speaking drill with YouTube Video. I repeatedly spoke 10 times for each sentence. It was a useful practice.

Frankly, I hadn't gotten around to follow this routine. It's a hassle or burden to me, so that I missed it some days. Plus, the more I proceed chapters, the longer the length of stories turned. Looks like it is designed. I've been swamped with speaking drills and incidential plenty of Anki cards!  

As you know, I can't complain, I learned a lot of native speaker's sentences from the book, and I used them even in this note. I'd say I want to give myself some credit because I picked out this book by deliberate searching.

But, the most important thing is that I completed this book. It got me thinking, should I start looking up other English text book? Well, at least, I won't be able to find the time for it before I handle whole Anki cards review!

I highly recommend this book for English learners. You can count on me!
