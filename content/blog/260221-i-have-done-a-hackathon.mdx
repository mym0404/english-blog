Since about 10 days ago, I started creating a product for a development hackathon. As today has been an era of AI agents for several fields, the topic of hackathon was planning and developing AI related product and one of the partnerships of the contest was OpenAI.

I figured out a product called MuseNote which helps Korean English learners improve their listening skills. When the learners are watching YouTube English, they are likely to repeat the same video all over again after getting opportunity for growth from teacher or AI.

MuseNote simplifies these procedures. It lets you tap the keyboard shortcuts during listening, records which sentences you couldn't understand and finally analysizes over that clues and creates AI generated report as a result of your listening.

We then might want to get feedbacks from the report and repeat the above steps until we conquer the video.

It took about 10 days for me to complete development. I've used AI agents extensively. Even though I spent a lot of tokens, the reason why I spent 10 days was it's imperative that I must create three systems such as Supabase backend, Next.js Web and Google Chrome Extension App.

Each system takes a crucial part in my overview. Thankfully, I have managed to submit my results to the hackathon, and I was satisfied with that.

I'm not pretty sure if I'll pass the first round, but I'm already proud of these works. I've learned not only how to explocit AI agents more strongly, but also how to develop Chrome Extension generally.
