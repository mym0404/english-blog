Yesterday, I dreamed in English for the first time. I can't remember whether I have had an English dream before, but as far as I know, this is my first time.

This calls for a celebration. The reason why I congratulate myself is that I've listened to a topic about dreams in English from my binge-listened English podcast on Spotify.

In dreams, there are few things I can do. In general, people can't control their dreams. What language is used in your dreams completely comes down to your subconscious processes. My experience goes to prove that English has been carved in my brain recently. It's such a good signal. Isn't it?

I only used a couple of simple sentences in the dream. It was a robbery situation where a criminal killed people in some place. I talked him down in English. Wow. Oh well, at least I can't remember if I succeeded **in stopping** him. Anyway, I couldn't care less about it for now.

I want to say that this is a concept which is linked to comprehensible input which is an educational terminology. I'm planning on practicing thinking in English day after day a bit more than I used to. I've been trying this. So, I'd say my efforts have paid off in some aspects.
