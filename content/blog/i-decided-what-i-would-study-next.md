I'm learning English from many sources. That includes a basic grammar book, the Spotify podcast, YouTube videos and an English learning app.

I felt I was not good at constructing sentences and wanted to improve that. I had researched a variety of books, but hadn't determined what I should read. Finally, I decided on the book I would study.

It is [가브리엘 잉글리시 다이어리](https://product.kyobobook.co.kr/detail/S000218052168). I know basic grammar is also important. But the reason I chose the storybook over a grammar book is that I have been watching the author's content on YouTube, and I've felt a sense of trust from her education ethics.

![image.png](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/mym0404/image-archive/20260106201709889.png)

It shares common concepts with the lecture I watched about two weeks ago. Her book seems to describe, in general, why Koreans have difficulty understanding native speakers' pronunciations and gives readers solutions to that problem.

I hope that this book will help me get better English writing skills. It absolutely depends on me, though!
