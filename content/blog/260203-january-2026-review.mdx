# Anki Flashcard Master

As time goes by, I can use Anki flashcard better than before. Reviewing my Anki cards is part of my daily routine, and it's fun for me. I've created plenty of cards, and fixed the format of cards.

I use ChatGPT not only to correct my sentences but also to create Anki cards. Currently, I'm managing three Anki decks which are quiz, sentence and vocabulary.

Moreover, I purchased HyperTTS tokens to attach TTS sounds to each card. It helps me hear correct pronunciation of words and sentences.

# Playing game with English

I've been playing Hollow Knight, a console game, on Nintendo Switch 2. The reason I wrote about it in this posting is that I'm playing it with English settings.

Initially, I hardly understood the words or grammar of the game. Because the words and grammatical structure of sentences in the game are somewhat old-fashioned for its mood, it was somewhat hard to read.

Given that, I didn't give up. When I met an unknown word or grammar, I stopped and created Anki cards repeatedly so far. It comes down to the fact that I only understand more than before for now.

But it's too much to understand whole sentences easily. If I hadn't played the game in English, I wouldn't have felt the mood of the game which the game designers intended.
