Since I turned 28, I've played a number of games on Nintendo Switch. Because I have pretty good memories about games of Nintendo not only Switch but NDS when I was young, I didn't hesitate to purchase Nitendo Switch 2.

> [!INFO] Info
> I used to play **Mario Kart NDS** or Rhythm Hero on the Nintendo NDS while I was on my way the math academy.

In this diary, I'm going to talk about **Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment** which I've beaten recently and received as a Christmas present by my girlfriend. Just so you know, I was not a big fan of the Zelda series. I haven't played Zelda games except **Breath of the Wild** and **Tears of the Kingdom**.

Frankly, I wasn't a Zelda person. But, the latest Zelda games on the Switch 2were fasctinating enough to make me play them. Specifically, their open-world play experience gives me such immersion. As a last warrior from the defeated kingdom, I could adventure anywhere and in ways whatever I wanted.

I can't leave out the **story** from the game. Rough but lingering stories and animations blew me away. Age of Imprisonment expands on the story between two latest serieses, a forgotten story about hidden heroes.


> [!NOTE] Playtime
> The total playtime was about 2~3 weeks. Over the weeks, I was able to enjoy it consistently.


If you are planning to play Age of Imprisonment, you have to play BOTW, TOK first. It gives you more immersion to play the game.

I wish I hadn't played these games yet; however, these experiences will be such great reminder of my youth in the future.

I appreciate you reading this. Thank you.
