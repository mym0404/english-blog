I've been into Overwatch lately. Overwatch is my favorite that I've been playing for a long while. I started playing Overwatch when I was 22.

I haven't enjoyed it consistently over the past few years, but I did enjoy it during that period more often than not.

There was a time when I reached a 4390 rating in Overwatch. My top heroes were Genji and Tracer. As I've gotten older and haven't been able to find the time for it lately, I'm around Master tier at this time.

I prefer to play a DPS role and have been in the mood to harass the enemy players who are in the backline.

I just got back from playing Overwatch today. I went out today for weekday leave.

It seems that people can't switch away from their all-time favorite game easily. Those who can't afford to play games all day don't have enough time to try and make an effort into various games.

For that reason, people usually end up sticking to their familiar games.

Anyway, Overwatch has suffered from a lot of setbacks in its management. The release of Overwatch 2 without many improvements clearly shows the hardships they have had to go through.

But at one point, I don't know exactly what happened, they changed. New heroes came out frequently and some useful updates were released. I don't think that was just luck. I'd say they have done a great job on that and want to give them some credit. Thank you.

If they hadn't stuck to moving it forward, it would have been spoiled entirely. 

I'm interested in collecting charming hero skins lately. For that, I need to stay focused on to achieve daily or weekly missions to get points somehow.

Anyway, Overwatch never gets old.
