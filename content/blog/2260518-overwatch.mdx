I've been into Overwatch lately. Overwatch is my favorite that I've been playing for a long while. I started playing Overwatch since I was 22.

I didn't enjoy it constantly over the past few years, but I got to enjoy it in that period more often than not.

There was a time when I reached a 4390 rating in Overwatch. My number one hero was Genzi and Tracer. As I've gotten older and couldn't spend time for it lately, I'm around Master tier at this time.

I'd prefer to play as a DPS role and have been in the mood to impedent the enemy players who was in the back position of the battlefield.

I had just gotten back from playing Overwatch even today. I went out today for monthly leave.

It seems that people can't make the switch easily in their all-time favorite game. Those who can't afford to play game all days don't have enough time to meet various games.

In light of that reason, people usually turn out sticking to their familiar games.

Anyway, Overwatch has suffered from a lot of setbacks in its management. The appearance of Overwatch 2 without any further improvement throughfully shows the hardship they has to go through.

But at one point, I don't know what happened exactly, they changed. New heroes came out frequently and some useful updates were released. I don't think that was just luck. I'd say they have done a great job on that and want to give them some credits. Thank you.

If they hadn't stuck to make it forward, It would have be spoiled entirely. 

I'm interested in collecting charm skins of several characters lately. For that, I need to stay focused on to archive daily or weekly missions to get points somehow.

Anyway, Overwatch never gets old.
