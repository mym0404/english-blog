I've gotten into Hades lately. It was added to my Steam library about 3 months ago, and I failed to understand the real beauty of it.

Since I had to begin with tedious weapons and no keepsakes, it was hard for me to get through some regions.

But I finally got to know why it's kind of a masterpiece now. I'm really in the mood to enjoy it til I complete full weapon or boon builds.

For some reason, the game became an enjoyable one at one point all of the sudden.

Maybe I think unlocking a range of weapons and getting the hang of progressing through stages with various builds of my own were the cause.

The longer I played it, the better my understanding of it grew. It's given me a lot of joy from playing it.

Furthermore, one of the bright sides is that I've been playing it in English language, so I've been able to learn tons of new vocabularies and unfamiliar grammar for me by all means.

I know that learning a foreign language is too stressful to stick to every day. But studying with the things you love can put a fun twist on it more an easier manner. Even though it may become harder to understand the story of the game for me, I can't complain.

I'm also so excited to play Hades 2. I can't wait to play it too. It makes me take a close look at Steam sales for Hades 2.

It's been a while since I picked out a game that is so good to enjoy all day. The greatest game ever!
