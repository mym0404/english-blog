I returned from my 5 days long vacation. I used to take three or four days of vacation, but I had a longer vacation this time.

This vacation included my girlfriend's birthday. I prepared some presents and reserved a couple of good places for that. Speaking of a place, one of my acquaintances lent me his place where I could see the Hangang river and gorgeous night sky.

On the other days of the vacation, I usually played the PC game and read a webtoon and a novel named "별을품은 소드마스터". That webtoon is a result of converting a novel into a webtoon. I read all the episodes from the beginning. It was so fascinating for me that I purchased all the novel volumes and read them all too.

Fantasy novels were my favorite in my childhood. When I was an elementary school student, I used to rent some fantasy novels from the bookstore and read them. I loved game fiction genre novels as much as fantasy.

By the time I read those books, I imagined I would be able to use a game capsule to connect my brain to a game world in the future. Even though there are some similar technologies like AR and VR, they aren't close to my dream yet. Can the thing that I don't construct be my dream? It sounds somehow funny.

By the way, even though it was 5 days, it was short for me. You know, the vacation is always short. There goes the time. However, for now, It doesn't disappoint me as much as before. As I'm returnning from vacations, my remaining vacations are less than before obviously. It means my discharge is coming up.
