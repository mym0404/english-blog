# Interview, Chat

Recently, I can't stop thinking about my next career.

As a developer who wanted to become a superman like [Jone Carmack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carmack), I sometimes think "is it the right fit for me?". Am I enough passionate to construct "The Great Thing"? What drives me shove myself into the deep immersion?

Finally, I decided that it's mandate to find a great leader who suggests the next destination, S-class teammates who can give me precise feedback, the truly immersive environment. I believe those help me feel motivated 24/7 on my work.

If you ask me, I'm not kind of selfish. As of me, entering a company and an office is meaning give-and-take stuff. If they give me something I want, I obliged to return more valuable inspiring stuff to them. Right?

From that context, I've been trying to have a soft spot for sharing great conversations with a few of developers, leaders, interviewers. Actually. It's so much fun. I can't get enough of it. I can do it all day long discussing frameworks and sharing ideas.

From there, I got into some new topics and got involved into them. I've learned a bunch of things and archieve some triumph there. Yup, I've been grown.
