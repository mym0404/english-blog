I ordered some English study books on the internet. One of them is an e-book and the others are physical books. As I mentioned [[260106-i-decided-what-i-would-study-next.mdx|in the previous post]], the e-book is the book which I wrote about that post. The other four books are grammar books. Three of them are part of a series called Grammar-Zone. The last one is about preposition and phrasal verb.

I'm excited about those books. The reason why I have bought five books at a once is that I had to use up my free points as soon as possible that I can use for buying books as a welfare of the military service. It was about ₩65,000. Last year, I didn't use it up entirely, and I decided to use all of it this year. By the way, I didn't miss it this time; it could have been worse.

The physical books will allow me to study them in the office regardless of whether I use the internet. Moreover, I wanted to pick out and study an English grammar book written in Korean someday. I'm really in the mood to read them.

Will I be able to get through all of them? I don't know yet. In spite of that, I feel like trying to study them step by step. I want to say that my English skill gets better while I learn new concepts from those materials. I appreciate it all.

I haven't decided what I'll read first. It might be a great achievement if I finish all of them. I'm looking forward to receiving those books. I will be writing reviews!
