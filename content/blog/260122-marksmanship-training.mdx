Today, I carried out my second marksmanship training, shooting training while I'm serving in the military. I want to show off the results. Even though this was my second time of training since I enlisted in the army, I did way better than the first session.

I got 16 out of 20 hits on the target, and I was also surprised by it. When I was in bootcamp of the army, I just got 2 out of 20. I couldn't remember the precise process of how to shoot using the rifle, but I managed to recall the names of parts and the mechanism of the rifle. We, the Korean army, use the K2 rifle as our main riple.

In the training building, there were a couple of superiors who controlled the overall training process. The atmosphere in the training area should stay tense but calm. If it weren't like that, it might cause fatal accidents.

As time went by, my turn came, I was also nervous about the fact that I had to shoot a real riple. If you've ever shot a gun, you know what I mean.

Anyway, after all, I managed to complete my training successfully and felt relieved. This experience had me recall the memories and mood of the Navy bootcamp, and It wasn't bad.
