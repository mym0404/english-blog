Nowadays, there are a lot of ai-agents have been released. I've picked up some of them and used for my personal side projects.

The final candidates are Opencode with Oh-My-Opencode and Codex from OpenAI. They are both robust and skillful agents which can help me write code aligning my intentions.

I haven't made up my mind until yesterday. Finally, I choose Codex. I'd rather play with faster development experience with agent and Codex was much faster than Opencode was.

Fundamentally, Opencode is kind of a multi-agent utility explains why it should be slower than other agents which utilize only one agent. Multi agent assistance has definitely their props but it wasn't for me.

The more I use Opencode, the slower it was. I couldn't help but use Codex in speed-intense workflows like simple modification. The trait of overthinking in Opencode kept me feeling upset sometimes.
