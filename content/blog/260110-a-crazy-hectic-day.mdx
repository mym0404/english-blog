January 10th was my January weekend pass in the army. I can leave my unit once a month. It was the weekend pass.

I had three appointments and had to go back and forth between Gyerong and Seoul by train. I arrived at Yongsan around 9:30. I met my girlfriend and went to a restaurant and a cafe one after another at Chungmuro.

There was no problem by then. The next appointment was my college senior, Giyeon Sin's wedding ceremony. I met and greeted some colleage acquaintances whom I hadn't been met for a long time.

The first thing that made me hectic was that I couldn't wait until the end of the wedding ceremony because I had to get to the next meeting in Gangnam by 3 pm(The wedding ceremony was started at 1:20, and it takes about 50 minutes to get from the hall to Gangnam).

In light of that, I couldn't be taken a picture with the groom, though. It was disappointing. I could eat only a single dish at the buffet and rushed to Gangnam. I couldn't even greet the groom. It was a shame.

Anyway, I managed to arrive at Gangnam without being late. The next appointment was having a deep chat with the maintainer of an opensource project I'm contributing to, [Sungyu Gang](https://github.com/gronxb).

We had a lot of intriguing and fascinating conversations on a variety of topics such as developments, developers, React Native and so on with a grab of beer and Korean whiskey with premium beef (He treated me XD).

I was surprised by how quickly I could talk. Rapping about topics, I wished that I could talk in English as fluent as that.

I was so motivated, and it boosted my self-esteem in development. It seems that was a result of both: he acknowledged my career and I could felt my intrinsic passion for development as much as before.

After the meeting, I had to return to Yongsan station to get on the train to Gyerong. Unfortunately, what I missed was that It was a snowy day.

As a result, transferring between subway lines wasn't smooth, and I got  stuck at the previous station, Noryangjin. My train was supposed to depart at 18:10, and I arrived at Yongsan at 18:13.

There wasn't the train which can bring me to Gyerong without being late anymore. I was really terrified, and my heart started racing.

In Noryangjin, I was almost typing a long apologize and help message to my superior. It was like "Are you having a comfort weekend? I'm really sorry about....". 

Fortunately, thanks to god, by rushing, I could catch the train. It turned out that the train was also delayed due to snow by about five minutes.

So, I'm in the barrack securely without any problem. 

---

This is today's story. There were a lot of things to be tankful for. I felt that a positive attitude matters in any circumstance. That attitude I've learned from some books was really helpful, and saved my life.

I hope you enjoy the rest of the day. Thank you.
