# Practice

> Why is testing yourself effective?

Testing myself is one of the active study methods. If we studied passive ways such as highlighting and reading, we can't clarify whether we know it correctly.

> Why can highlighting feel helpful but mislead you?

As I mentioned, highlighting is a passive study method. We can highlight some difficult elements. But it won't remain in our brains. It gives us a false sense of competence.

> What is interleaving and why might it work?

Interleaving is a study method where we mix various study materials together. It causes our brains to switch from one that is more prioritzed for a subject to others.

> Why does spacing help learning?

Spacing itself doesn't do much. The keystone is sleeping. During sleep, our brains arrange and construct long-term and strong memories. Spcaing ensures that we get enough sleep.

> What was the key difference between the two resident groups?
